Key rules

  • English text is prepared for conversion.
  • The text is converted into the Kamagochin writing form.
  • Words starting with a vowel get no extra A.
  • For a consonant-starting word, add A after the first mapped character.
  • If the coded word begins HJA, TSA, QAI or XHA, add A after that complete sequence.

How it works

  1. Process the English text.
  2. Convert the text into a Latin-letter form.
  3. Apply the Kamagochin substitutions and word-start rule.
